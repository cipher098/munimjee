## Initial semantic dispositions

The fact classifier is deliberately small: some English and transliterated Hindi words are accepted; Devanagari questions generally hand off. The renderer emits English. Safe handoff is a factual-safety behavior, not evidence of native-language answer quality or complete multilingual support.

The classifier accepts `material`/`materials`, while the public observation and renderer carry product name, SKU option labels, price and stock only. A missing-material check must accept either human handoff or explicit material-unknown/unrecorded wording with confirmation/clarification. Requiring an exact hard-coded handoff sentence would be unnecessarily narrow. A generic catalogue dump ending with “Please confirm your preferred option with our team” does not identify material uncertainty. If reproduced, classify this as a scoped response/actionability gap (P2), not an invented material, price, stock, cross-tenant disclosure or financial-action hard-gate failure.

Unknown and ambiguous explicit references currently return no reply and no review reason at the WA decision seam. The complete ingress preserves reference status for the existing seller queue, but that full journey is outside the new six episodes. Do not label silence as successful clarification or human takeover. IG `CatalogueAuthorityHandoff` is a withholding exception; durable receipt/error/operator visibility must be established through its actual worker/operator path, not the exception alone.
