What this dashboard cannot measure yet

The order history below cannot reconstruct past visits, views or checkout starts. Observed activity above has its own coverage and context-start dates. Attribution, completed refunds, payment fees and net revenue remain unavailable. Order dates do not tell us when money settled.

Only storefront orders are included. Orders recorded only in older conversations and other payment evidence are separate. COD and online orders without a gateway snapshot have no reliable test marker, so test activity may remain.
