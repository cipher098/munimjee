Request data deletion

To request deletion of your Munimjee account or personal data processed through our connected Instagram or WhatsApp services, email tech@munimjee.in with the subject “Munimjee data deletion request”.

  1. Identify your seller account using its registered email or connected business account. If you are a customer of a seller, identify the seller and the WhatsApp number or Instagram account used to contact them.
  2. Tell us whether you want to delete your account, a conversation, or other specified personal data.
  3. Our support team may request information needed to verify your identity and locate the records. Do not send passwords, API tokens, OTPs or payment-card details.

Requests are handled by our support team. We will confirm the scope and any applicable retention exceptions under our Privacy Policy.

Disconnecting and opting out

Disconnecting an integration stops its use in Munimjee; it is separate from requesting deletion of stored records. Sellers can disconnect WhatsApp in Social → Connections. Customers can reply STOP to a connected seller’s WhatsApp automation to stop further automated messages. STOP does not delete historical records.

For privacy questions, contact tech@munimjee.in.
